Rajshahi city: the Silk City on the bank of the Padma river

Rajshahi is a major city in north-western Bangladesh on the bank of the Padma river and the capital of Rajshahi Division. It is widely known as the Silk City, and the region is famous for mangoes. Varendra University is located here.
