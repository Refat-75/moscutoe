Varendra University: approved in 2012, campus at Rajshahi Bypass Road

Varendra University was approved by the Ministry of Education on 14 March 2012 and by the UGC on 20 March 2012, and classes began on 24 September 2012. The campus is at Rajshahi Bypass Road, Chandrima, Paba, Rajshahi-6204, and it has four schools: Business and Law, Arts and Social Science, Engineering, and Science and Technology.
